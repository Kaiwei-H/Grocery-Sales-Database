# Analysis Performed

The project includes several analytical queries.

### Sales Overview

Total transactions, units sold and revenue.

### Monthly Revenue Trend

Tracks sales performance over time.

### Top Products

Identifies best-selling products by revenue and units sold.

### Category Performance

Measures revenue contribution by product category.

### Customer Analysis

- Top customers by revenue
- Customer segmentation by revenue level
- Repeat customer rate

### Salesperson Performance

Compares revenue contribution by sales staff.

### Discount Impact

Analyzes the effect of discounting on transaction value.

### Revenue Growth

Uses window functions to calculate monthly revenue growth.

# Key Insights

Example findings from the analysis:

- Revenue shows a generally increasing monthly trend.
- A relatively small number of products generate a large share of revenue.
- Customer revenue distribution is highly concentrated among top buyers.
- Discounted transactions generate significant revenue but vary by discount band.
- Repeat customers represent an important share of total revenue.
